title: Void of Course
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book II chapter 3

The label void of course is younger than Picatrix, and the translation this almanac draws on never uses the phrase. What the app means by it is later astrology's term for the Moon running on through the rest of her sign after her last aspect to another planet, making no new one before she crosses into the next sign. What Picatrix supplies is the doctrine underneath the label. In every work, he says, heed the Moon, for she outweighs every other planet: she is the mediatrix who draws down the influences of the stars and pours them out upon the world below. He requires her to be well positioned and clear of Saturn, Mars, and their aspects, free of the Sun's combustion, tied to the fortunes by the trine and the sextile, and separate from one fortunate planet while joined to another, so that her power always has a source and a destination. A Moon running void is simply a Moon failing that last requirement: nothing receives from her, and she is joined to nothing. This almanac's reading of the doctrine is the source's own counsel, to wait until she is joined again before founding anything.
