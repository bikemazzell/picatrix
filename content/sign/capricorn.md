title: Capricorn
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book III chapter 2

For Capricorn, while the Sun is in Cancer, the operator enters a house with room for only two people, kept seven days under a covering of fresh odoriferous branches renewed each day, the whole while suffumigated with aloewood and incense. Dressed in his finest red clothing, he uncovers the house, speaks the recorded words, walks around it seventy times, suffumigates it for an hour with aloewood in a silver censer, walks the seventy circuits again, and sacrifices the cock before going back inside, where a seated man waits to be conjured by the Moon, beautiful and bright, before the request is stated. The chapter attaches two stories to the rite: a friend won back a servant girl he had sold and come to love, the working turning her new owner's heart against her until he sold her back at a modest price; and a man who performed the ritual on a night wholly unsuited to it had his mouth sealed by what appeared to him and lived forty hours in the greatest terror, ending completely ruined. The passage closes with the rule that fits the whole chapter: the operator must be inclined toward good works, holy, pure, and removed from temptation.
