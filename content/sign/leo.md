title: Leo
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book IV chapter 2

For Leo the rite moves to an uninhabited place. Seven censers stand in a ring on the ground, one cubit from its nearest neighbor, a goose egg inscribed with figures between each pair, and the operator works in a yellow woolen cloth bound on his head and a yellow woolen shirt, his face always turned toward the Moon, a thick tin needle three palms long in his right hand. A cock with its crest cut off is killed in the middle of the ring under the same bone-and-no-iron rule as in Aries, and its pieces burn in the censers; its blood, taken on the head of the needle, is dripped onto every censer and egg. Counting two censers from the one before him, the operator takes the egg beyond them, pierces it through with the needle, and suffumigates it with frankincense and yellow sandalwood while speaking the recorded words fifteen times. The man of the apparition then comes, and the chapter says the request is fulfilled at once, with effect.
