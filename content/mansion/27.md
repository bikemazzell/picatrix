title: Al Fargh al-Thani
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twenty-seventh mansion covers the middle of Pisces, from about the fourth degree and seventeenth minute to the seventeenth degree and ninth minute. Book I gives it commerce, unity, harvests, and healing on the good side, against ruined wealth, impeded building, imperiled sailing, prolonged imprisonment, and plain harm. Book IV gives its image to the destruction of a bathhouse.

## Good For

- Commerce and its profits
- Uniting associates
- Harvests, which it increases
- Healing infirmities

## Not Good For

- A man's riches, which it ruins
- Construction, which it impedes
- Those sailing, whom it imperils
- Captives, whose incarceration it prolongs

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a winged man holding an empty, pierced vessel raised to his mouth, formed from red earth and cooked hard over a fire; asafoetida and liquid storax go into the vessel, and the whole figure is buried in the bathhouse it is meant to ruin. The source says the bath is cursed thereby and bathers abandon it. Of all the images in Book IV this is the pettiest, and the most vivid.
