title: Al-Ghafr
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The fifteenth mansion opens Libra and runs to about the twelfth degree and fifty-second minute of the Scales. Book I gives it two goods that sit low in the earth, wells and buried treasure, and stacks a heavy list of harms above them: impeded journeys, divided marriages and friendships, scattered enemies, and destroyed homes. Book IV answers with friendship and goodwill.

## Good For

- Digging wells
- Seeking buried treasure

## Not Good For

- Travelers, whose journeys it impedes
- The marriage bed, which it divides
- Friendship, for it sows discord among friends and associates
- A standing enemy's position and home, which it scatters and destroys

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a seated man with a paper, posed as a reader, cut in enque, a glassy material the translation notes without full certainty. The suffumigation is incense and nutmeg, and the figure is carried on the person. The operator asks for the things that join: friendship, love, and goodwill, in the words of the source.
