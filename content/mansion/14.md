title: Al-Simak
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The fourteenth mansion closes Virgo, running from about the seventeenth degree and ninth minute to the sign's last degree. Book I gives it love, healing done with knowledge of the body and its medicine, strong kings, safe sailing, and friendship, with cursed harvests as its single harmful work. Book IV sets against all this an image for dividing a couple.

## Good For

- Love between man and wife
- Healing the infirm, where the operator knows the body and its medicine
- Kings, that they grow strong and win the throne
- Those sailing
- The friendship of associates

## Not Good For

- Harvests and plants, which it curses

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a dog with its own tail in its mouth, cast in red bronze, suffumigated with dog hair and cat hair, and buried wherever the operator wishes the division to work. The operator names the man and woman to be disjoined through enmity and ill will. The same mansion that Book I gives to healing and marriage carries, in Book IV, the working that pulls them apart.
