title: Al-Jabhah
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The tenth mansion runs from the last third of Cancer into Leo, ending near the eighth degree and thirty-fourth minute of the Lion. Its elections in Book I lean domestic and civic: marriage, buildings, goodwill, and mutual help, with the imprisonment of enemies close by. Book IV gives its talisman to healing, and to easing the pains of childbirth.

## Good For

- Love between man and wife
- Buildings, which it reinforces and completes
- Goodwill between associates, and helping one another

## Not Good For

- Enemies and travelers, whom it curses
- Captives, whose jails it reinforces

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a lion's head in gold or brass, bearing the lord's name, suffumigated with amber. Its work is medicine: the image is given to a sick man to carry, or washed in a liquid that is then given to drink, and the source extends the same remedy to a woman in the pains of childbirth. Of all the mansion talismans, this is the one most plainly turned to cure rather than curse.
