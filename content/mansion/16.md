title: Al-Zubana
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The sixteenth mansion covers the second half of Libra, from about the twelfth degree and fifty-second minute to the twenty-fifth degree and forty-second minute. Book I stacks curses here: on merchandise, harvests, plants, marriages, and friendships, on a desired woman, and on journeys, with the freeing of prisoners as its one act of mercy. Book IV flips the whole mansion to profit.

## Good For

- Freeing captives from their jail cells

## Not Good For

- Merchandise, harvests, and plants, which it curses
- Concord among friends, and between man and wife
- A desired woman, whom the source works to curse
- Journeys, which it works to make fail

## Talisman

The Book IV image for profit in buying and selling is a man seated on a throne holding scales in his hands, engraved on a silver lamella. The suffumigation is a fragrant one, and the figure is shown to the stars on seven successive nights while the request is spoken over it. The throne and the scales sit squarely against Book I's stack of curses, the clearest single reversal between the two chapters.
