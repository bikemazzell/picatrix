title: Al-Tarf
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The ninth mansion covers the second half of Cancer, from about the twelfth degree and fifty-second minute to the twenty-fifth degree and forty-second minute. Book I gives it almost unbroken to hard uses; its one defensible work is defense itself, the turning back of those who mean harm. Book IV hangs the harshest image in its book on this mansion.

## Good For

- Standing against those who seek to do harm, whom the source curses here
- Forcing an aggressor onto the defensive

## Not Good For

- Harvests, which it condemns
- Travelers, whom it curses
- Alliances, which it splits with divisions and enmities

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a lead figure of a man without genitals, hands covering his eyes, with the lord's name written on his neck, suffumigated with pine resin. The source directs it at making a named person fall ill. This almanac records the image as part of the history of the text and offers no instruction in its use.
