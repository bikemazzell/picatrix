title: Al-Nathrah
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The eighth mansion takes the first half of Cancer, from the first degree of the sign to about the twelfth degree and fifty-second minute. Book I mixes affection with captivity: love and safe wagon travel on one side, strong jails and curses on the other, with a household blessing between them. Book IV lifts the same mansion to victory.

## Good For

- Love and friendship
- Those going through villages in wagons, that they proceed safely
- Friendship between two associates
- Houses, for it drives mice and bedbugs out of wherever they infest

## Not Good For

- Captives, whose cells it reinforces and whom it curses and afflicts

## Talisman

The Book IV image is an eagle-faced man, cast in tin with the lord's name written on his chest, and suffumigated, oddly for so watery a mansion, with sulfur. Held before an army, it is said to give conquest and victory to the side that carries it. The spread of this mansion, from bedbugs to battle, is one of the widest in the list.
