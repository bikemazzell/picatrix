title: Al-Shaulah
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The nineteenth mansion runs from the last third of Scorpio into Sagittarius, ending near the fourth degree and seventeenth minute of the Archer. Book I gives it the longest and most violent election list of the twenty-eight: cities surrounded, entered, and plundered, wealth ruined, men driven from their places, ships shattered, and captives killed or made to flee, with wagon travelers benefited and harvests increased between them. Book IV gives its talisman to women's blood and childbirth.

## Good For

- Wagon travelers passing between villages
- Harvests, which it increases
- Captives seeking escape, for it makes them flee

## Not Good For

- Cities, which armies surround, enter, and plunder
- The wealth of any man the operator names
- Men's dwellings, from which it drives them out
- Ships, which it destroys and shatters
- The riches of associates
- Captives at the mercy of the operator, whom it kills

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a woman with a hand raised before her face, cut in zinc, which the text describes as a kind of bronze, and suffumigated with liquid storax. Its stated work is to bring on the menstruation of a named woman; bound to the rear of a woman in labor, the same figure is said to deliver the child easily and without danger. From the burning cities of Book I to the delivery room of Book IV, no mansion in the list swings further.
