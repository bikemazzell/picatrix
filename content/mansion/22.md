title: Sa'd al-Dhabih
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twenty-second mansion opens Capricorn, running from the first degree of the sign to about the twelfth degree and fifty-second minute. Book I gives it healing and escape side by side with discord: the infirm healed, slaves and captives made to flee, goodwill among associates, and two men set at odds. Book IV gives its ring to the binding of tongues.

## Good For

- Healing infirmities
- Goodwill between associates
- Slaves and captives, whom it makes flee and escape

## Not Good For

- Concord between two men, whom it sets at odds

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a helmeted man with wings on his feet, cut on an iron ring and suffumigated with quicksilver. Its work is twofold: to bind the tongue of a named person lest they curse the bearer, and to keep safe those who flee from their enemies, for whom the ring is worn. When the binding is spoken, the wearer seals black wax with the ring.
