title: Al-Sarfah
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twelfth mansion runs from the last third of Leo into the opening of Virgo, ending near the fourth degree and seventeenth minute of the Maiden. Book I gives it farm goods and financial ruin side by side, and strengthens those who live under authority. Book IV turns its image against lovers.

## Good For

- Harvests and plants, which it improves
- The standing of allies, authorities, captives, and slaves, keeping them firm and prospering

## Not Good For

- A man's finances, which the source works to ruin
- Ships, which it curses

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a dragon locked in struggle with a man, cast in black lead with the lord's name written straight upon it. The suffumigation is a strange pairing, dog hair mixed with cat hair, and the image is buried in whatever place the separation is meant to reach. Against Book I's standing crops and prospering servants, the lead dragon reads as the mansion's shadow side, and the source does not reconcile the two.
