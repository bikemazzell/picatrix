title: Al-Dhira
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The seventh mansion closes Gemini, running from about the seventeenth degree and ninth minute to the sign's last degree. Book I makes it a merchant's mansion, favorable to trade, profit, harvests, and audiences with the great, with one spiteful work tucked among the goods. Book IV returns it to every good without reservation.

## Good For

- Trade and profit
- Safe travel, by land and by sea
- Increasing harvests
- Friendship among friends and associates
- Going before a king or a great lord, gaining his favor, and getting what you wish from him
- Keeping flies out of a place

## Not Good For

- A man's career and standing, which the source works to ruin

## Talisman

The Book IV sigil is silver and bears a robed figure reaching both hands toward heaven in the posture of one praying and supplicating, with the lord's name written on his chest. The suffumigation is simply a good-smelling one, the request is asked from the things that pertain to the good, and the bearer carries the sigil until the matter ensues. Of all the mansion images, this is the most plainly devotional.
