title: Al-Zubrah
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The eleventh mansion spans the middle of Leo, from about the eighth degree and thirty-fourth minute to the twenty-first degree and twenty-fifth minute. Book I makes it public-spirited: freedom for captives, organized trade, protected travelers, stable buildings, and growing wealth for one's friends, with armies sent against open cities as its one act of war. Book IV gives it glory and dread.

## Good For

- Liberating captives
- Organizing trade and its profits
- Travelers on the road
- Buildings, for stability
- Increasing the wealth of friends

## Not Good For

- Open cities and villages, for armies are positioned outside them

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a man mounted on a lion, engraved on a golden tablet, spear in the right hand and left hand over the lion's ear, the mansion's name written in a straight line across the figure. The operator asks for glory: to be feared by men, to have everyone tremble at beholding him, and to be received well and honored by kings, lords, and noblemen. No suffumigation is named for this image; the tablet is simply carried, and the source says what was sought will be.
