title: Sa'd Bula
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twenty-third mansion covers the second half of Capricorn, from about the twelfth degree and fifty-second minute to the twenty-fifth degree and forty-second minute. Book I repeats a short list: healing, friendship united, a couple divided, and prisoners freed. Book IV turns the same mansion to devastation.

## Good For

- Healing infirmities
- Uniting friends
- Captives, who escape from their jail cells

## Not Good For

- A couple's concord, for it divides a man from his wife

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a dog-headed cat, cut in an iron sigil and suffumigated with dog hair. It is shown to the stars while the mansion is in the ascendant, and on the following night it is buried in the place marked out for destruction. The short, half-kindly election list of Book I gives no warning of the image Book IV hangs on this mansion.
