title: Al-Iklil
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The seventeenth mansion runs from the last third of Libra into Scorpio, ending near the eighth degree and thirty-fourth minute of the Scorpion. Book I gives it a double face: deception, sieges, and stability together with safe travel on water, and it adds a doctrine found nowhere else in the list. Friendships forged while the Moon is in this mansion endure unbroken, and everything belonging to the arrangement of lasting love belongs here.

## Good For

- Friendships meant to last, which the source says endure unbroken
- The arrangement of lasting love
- Buildings, which it reinforces and stabilizes
- Those navigating on water

## Not Good For

- Honest dealing, for it heightens deceptions that others may excel through
- Open cities, which armies are positioned around

## Talisman

The Book IV image guards a house against thieves: a monkey with its hand raised above its shoulder, cut on an iron sigil, suffumigated with monkey hair and the hair of a female mouse, wrapped in the monkey's own pelt, and buried in the house. The source says neither thief nor evil can cross the threshold thereafter. The enduring friendships of Book I and the watchful monkey of Book IV agree better than most pairs in the list.
