title: Al-Dabaran
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The fourth mansion covers the middle of Taurus, from about the eighth degree and thirty-fourth minute to the twenty-first degree and twenty-fifth minute. Book I loads it with the year's harsher works, and Book IV agrees by giving its talisman to enmity. What it grants is narrow and firm: treasure brought to light, and vermin stopped.

## Good For

- Finding buried treasure
- Binding or killing reptiles and poisonous animals

## Not Good For

- Cities, villages, and other buildings, which it works to condemn
- The bond between a master and his servant
- Concord between husband and wife
- Wells and springs, which the source says may be contaminated under it

## Talisman

The Book IV image for creating enmity is a soldier mounted on a horse, a snake held in the right hand, formed from red wax, with red myrrh and storax for the suffumigation. The operator asks the image for enmity, division, and ill feeling between named parties, and the text says it comes to pass. This almanac records the assignment as the source gives it; the election belongs to the medieval list, not to any recommendation of this app.
