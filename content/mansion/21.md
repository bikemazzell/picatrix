title: Al-Baldah
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twenty-first mansion closes Sagittarius, running from about the seventeenth degree and ninth minute to the sign's last degree. Book I gives it steady goods: buildings reinforced, harvests increased, profits held firmly, and protection for those traveling through villages, with one work of marital separation set against them. Book IV gives its image to destruction outright.

## Good For

- Reinforcing buildings
- Increasing harvests
- Retaining profits firmly
- Those traveling through villages

## Not Good For

- A marriage, for it draws a wife away from her husband

## Talisman

The Book IV image for destroying is a man with two faces, one before and one behind, suffumigated with sulfur and carob. The figure is placed in a small bronze container together with sulfur, carob, and some hairs, and buried wherever the operator wishes the desolation to fall. The steady accumulator of Book I becomes, in Book IV, the emptied place its name has always suggested.
