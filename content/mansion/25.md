title: Sa'd al-Akhbiyah
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twenty-fifth mansion covers the middle of Aquarius, from about the eighth degree and thirty-fourth minute to the twenty-first degree and twenty-fifth minute. Book I gives it the heaviest instrumental list after the nineteenth: armies, vengeance, hastened messengers, severed marriages, withered harvests, bound bodies, and reinforced prisons, with buildings its one plain benefit. Book IV turns the same mansion to the protection of crops.

## Good For

- Buildings
- Envoys and messengers, whom it speeds on their way and brings home quickly

## Not Good For

- Cities, which armies are positioned around
- Enemies, on whom vengeance and evil are worked at will
- A marriage, which it separates
- The marriage bed, which it binds
- Any limb of the body, which it can render useless
- Harvests, which it withers
- Captives, whose prisons it reinforces

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a man planting trees, carved on a sigil of fig wood, suffumigated with the flowers of those same trees, and set beside a tree where protection is wanted. The source says no harm will come to that place as long as the image remains there. That the witherer of harvests and the planter of trees share one mansion is the source's own irony, not this almanac's.
