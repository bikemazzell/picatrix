title: Al-Sharatain
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The first mansion opens the zodiac, running from the first degree of Aries to about the twelfth degree and fifty-second minute of the same sign. Picatrix presents it through the sages of India, who treated the twenty-eight mansions as the foundation of every election. This is where their list begins, and it carries the doubled character of a beginning: safe passage and medicine on one side, rupture on the other.

## Good For

- Journeys, which the sages of India began under this mansion
- Taking medicine
- Images for travelers, so they come and go safely

## Not Good For

- Concord between husband and wife, or between two friends, for the source works discord here
- Partnerships between associates
- Keeping a servant, since it makes slaves flee

## Talisman

Book IV gives the first mansion over to destroying and depopulating. Its image is a dark figure cloaked in a garment of hair, upright with the spear of a warrior held in the right hand. The maker casts it on an iron ring, suffumigates the ring with liquid storax, and seals black wax with it while stating the work; the text names the lord of the mansion, but writes the name in garbled characters this almanac does not reproduce. Around the whole list of mansions Picatrix sets one standing rule, worth quoting once here: in every beneficial work the Moon must stand well placed, clear of Saturn and Mars and out of the Sun's beams, while works of harm assume the reverse.
