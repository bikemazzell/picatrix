title: Al-Awwa
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The thirteenth mansion covers the first half of Virgo, from about the fourth degree and seventeenth minute to the seventeenth degree and ninth minute. Book I fills it with plain civic goods: trade, harvests, roads, buildings, freed prisoners, and the favor of noblemen. It is the only mansion in the chapter whose election list holds no destructive work at all.

## Good For

- Trade and its profits
- Harvests, which it enlarges
- Travelers by road
- Completing buildings
- Freeing the incarcerated
- Charming noblemen to one's benefit

## Not Good For

- Book I assigns no harmful work to this mansion
- Its benefits still hang on the standing rule: the Moon well positioned, clear of Saturn, Mars, and the Sun's beams

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a pair of wax figures, a red man and a white woman, formed in an embrace, bathed in rosewater, wrapped in white silk, and suffumigated with aloewood and amber, with the name of the intended man written on either figure. Its stated work is love between man and woman, and the source adds a clinical use: a man bound by a spell and unable to lie with a woman is to carry the images, by which he is loosened. The frankest of the mansion talismans belongs to the gentlest of the election lists.
