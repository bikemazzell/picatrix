title: Al Fargh al-Awwal
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twenty-sixth mansion runs from the last third of Aquarius into Pisces, ending near the fourth degree and seventeenth minute of the Fishes. Book I gives it a short, mixed list: men united in mutual esteem, carriage travelers protected, prisons reinforced, and evil worked on the prisoners inside them. Book IV gives its image to love.

## Good For

- Uniting men in mutual esteem
- Those traveling by carriage

## Not Good For

- Captives, whose prisons and cells it reinforces while working evil upon them

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a woman with her hair unbound, holding a container before her as if she meant to place her hair in it, formed from white wax and mastic gum melted together. The suffumigation is of sweet-smelling things; the figure is kept in a small container with other fragrant material and carried. The operator asks for the love and friendship of a named woman, and the source says the desire is accomplished.
