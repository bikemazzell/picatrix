title: Al-Qalb
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The eighteenth mansion covers the middle of Scorpio, from about the eighth degree and thirty-fourth minute to the twenty-first degree and twenty-fifth minute. Book I gives it conspiracy against kings and vengeance on enemies alongside plain building and the freeing of prisoners. Book IV makes its talisman a healer of fever and a ward against serpents.

## Good For

- Building, and reinforcing buildings
- Freeing captives from prison cells

## Not Good For

- Kings, against whom it rouses conspiracy
- Enemies of the operator, on whom it inflicts vengeance
- Friendships, which it divides

## Talisman

The Book IV image is an adder with its tail raised over its head, cast in bronze, suffumigated with stag's horn. Buried in a vase beneath the house, it is said to keep out serpents and every other harmful creature; carried on the person, the same figure is held to remove fever and abdominal pain at once. The heart of the scorpion turns out to serve medicine more than malice.
