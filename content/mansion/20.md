title: Al-Na'am
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twentieth mansion covers the middle of Sagittarius, from about the fourth degree and seventeenth minute to the seventeenth degree and ninth minute. Book I gives it to taming and gathering: wild beasts domesticated, travelers returning swiftly, any man drawn to whatever place the operator wishes, and good men associated with one another, with prisons reinforced and friends' wealth ruined in the margins. Book IV makes it the hunter's mansion.

## Good For

- Domesticating wild and untamed beasts
- Swift returns for those traveling by carriage
- Drawing any man to wherever you wish him to come
- Associating good men with one another

## Not Good For

- Captives, whose prison cells it reinforces
- The riches of friends, on which it brings evil and ruin

## Talisman

The Book IV image for hunting on land is an archer's figure on a tin tablet: a man's head and hands on the body of a horse, four-footed and tailed, holding a bow. Fox hair makes the suffumigation, and the hunter carries the tablet with him. The source promises that all the land's game comes to the bearer immediately, to be caught at will.
