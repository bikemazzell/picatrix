title: Sa'd al-Su'ud
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The twenty-fourth mansion crosses from the last third of Capricorn into Aquarius, ending near the eighth degree and thirty-fourth minute of the Water Bearer. Book I gives it commerce, goodwill in marriage, and victory for soldiers, against ruined friendships and disrupted enterprises. Book IV gives its talisman to herds and flocks.

## Good For

- Commerce and its profits
- Goodwill between husband and wife
- Soldiers, giving them victory over enemies

## Not Good For

- The riches of friends, which it ruins
- Enterprises, which it disrupts so they never come to fruition

## Talisman

The Book IV image is a nursing mother, her child held to her breast, carved on the well-cleaned horn of a castrated ram, and suffumigated with the scrapings taken off the horn in its cleaning. Hung at the throat of a ram of the flock, it is said to grow the herd and keep mortality from it; for cattle, the same figure is made from a bovine horn and hung at the bull's throat. The most domestic of the images carries the most pastoral of the works.
