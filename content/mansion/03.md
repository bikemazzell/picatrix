title: Al-Thurayya
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The third mansion crosses from the last stretch of Aries into Taurus, ending near the eighth degree and thirty-fourth minute of the Bull. The source names it openly as the Pleiades. Its works gather around protection and craft: sailors, fire, and the hunt, with reconciliation at home and one hard use against captives.

## Good For

- Those sailing by sea, that they return safely
- Works of alchemy, and any work done with fire
- Hunting expeditions on land
- Affection between husband and wife

## Not Good For

- The hope of captives, for it reinforces their cells

## Talisman

Book IV calls this mansion the acquisition of every good. Its figure is a seated, dressed woman with her right hand lifted above her head, cut on a square-faced silver ring that is worn on the finger. The suffumigation is a rich one: musk, camphor, mastic gum, and the aromatic powder the translation renders as hooves. The request is spoken over the figure, and the source promises it will be carried into effect.
