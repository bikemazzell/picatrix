title: Al-Haqa
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The fifth mansion runs from the last third of Taurus into the opening of Gemini, ending near the fourth degree and seventeenth minute of the Twins. Book I conditions its kindest works on the Moon rising in a human sign, and lists the human signs as Gemini, Virgo, Libra, Sagittarius, and Aquarius. It is a mansion of apprenticeship and safe roads.

## Good For

- Children taking up a craft or trade
- Travelers on journeys, that they return quickly
- Those traveling by sea
- Improving buildings
- Affection between husband and wife

## Not Good For

- The alliance of two men, which the source works to dissolve

## Talisman

Book IV gives the fifth mansion to being well received by kings and officials. Its sigil is silver, sculpted with a man's head alone, no body attached; the maker writes the lord's name on the head and his request anywhere on the sigil, suffumigates it with sandalwood, and carries it with him. The same sigil, placed under the head at night with the desire held firmly in mind, is said to return the answer in sleep. The lord's name appears in the translation only as garbled characters and is not reproduced here.
