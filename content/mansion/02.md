title: Al-Butain
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The second mansion occupies the second half of Aries, from about the twelfth degree and fifty-second minute to the twenty-fifth degree and forty-second minute of the sign. Its elections in Book I are a digger's and a farmer's list: wells, buried treasure, and fields of wheat, with the unmaking of buildings set against them. The works sit low in the ground.

## Good For

- Digging channels and wells
- Discovering lost and buried treasure
- Planting a mass of wheat

## Not Good For

- Finished buildings, which the source works to destroy
- Peace between two men, for it sets one raging against the other
- The ease of prisoners, since it makes their cells stronger and more secure

## Talisman

Book IV turns the same stretch of sky to gentler use: the removal of anger. The maker blends white wax with mastic gum over a fire, lets the mixture cool, and from it forms the image of a crowned king, which he keeps on his person after suffumigating it with aloewood. Where Book I tightens a prison under this mansion, Book IV cools a temper; Picatrix holds both assignments without apology, and this almanac sets them side by side.
