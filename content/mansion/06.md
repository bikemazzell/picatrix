title: Al-Hana
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book I chapter 4 and Book IV chapter 9

## Essence

The sixth mansion spans the first half of Gemini, from about the fourth degree and seventeenth minute to the seventeenth degree and ninth minute. Book I fills it with sieges and blighted fields, but keeps two plain goods: friendship between associates and the hunt in the countryside. Book IV counters with one of its warmest images, a talisman for love between two people.

## Good For

- Friendship between two associates
- Hunting in the countryside

## Not Good For

- Cities and villages, which the source works to destroy
- Their defenders, for armies are positioned around them
- Kings, whose enemies exact vengeance under it
- Harvests and trees, which it condemns
- Medicines, which it curses so they fail on those who take them

## Talisman

The Book IV image for love is a pair: two figures of white wax, formed in an embrace and wrapped together in white silk, suffumigated with aloewood and amber. The operator asks that two named people be joined, with friendship and love placed between them. Set against Book I's sieges, the embracing pair shows how one stretch of sky could carry opposite assignments in different chapters of the source.
