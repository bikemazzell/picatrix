title: Mercury Approaching Retrograde
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book II chapter 3 and Book III chapter 1

The three-stage shadow this almanac draws around each retrograde is modern bookkeeping; Picatrix knows the impediment of retrogradation but draws no calendar around its approach, and this almanac's stage names are its own. The source's rule is prospective in exactly this direction, though: a planet set as lord of the ascendant or of the request must not be retrograde, for a retrograde lord leaves the matter dragging, stretched out, and unfinished, and effects founded under such a condition are ruined from the start and reach their end only through effort and risk. Mercury is intellect's planet, of learning, dialectic, geometry, astronomy, writing, and the reading of tongues, and his benevolence suits messengers and scribes. When he slows toward a station, this almanac's reading of the doctrine is to finish Mercury's business before the station rather than plant anything there.
