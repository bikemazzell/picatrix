title: Waxing Moon
source: Picatrix (adapted), Book II chapter 3

Picatrix puts the doctrine in one breath: find her gaining light, and her strength and power are enhanced toward everything you wish to perform that concerns augmentation; find her losing it, and she is apt and congruent for the tasks of diminution. The superior state adds speed to light: gaining, quick in her course, and growing in reckoning is the Moon at her best for any work meant to grow and last, and the quickest condition of all is a fortunate planet ascending while she is on the increase in both light and account. The whole waxing half, from her separation from the Sun through the squares to the opposition, is the friendlier ground for elections, favorable to sales and purchases, to advice, opinions, and duties, and to disputes. Two cautions ride with it: a waxing Moon facing Mars by aspect is counted a misfortune rather than a fortune, and the light she gains is the light whose loss, later, the doctrine reads as harm, delay, and impediment.
